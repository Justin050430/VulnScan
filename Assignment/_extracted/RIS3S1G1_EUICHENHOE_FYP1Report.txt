Abstract 
The project addresses the critical issue of unauthorized email account access caused by weak or 
stolen passwords, which contribute to 81% of hacking-related breaches. Often, users are unaware 
of account takeovers until significant damage has occurred. To tackle this, the proposed solution, 
"Password Cracking Detection and Prevention System for Login Page with Anomalous Device 
Detection and Response," is designed to enhance security for both individual and organizational 
users. The system works by utilizing advanced real-time anomaly detection algorithms to identify 
suspicious login activities, particularly from unfamiliar devices. When such activities are detected, 
it immediately alerts users via email, providing actionable options to log out of all active sessions 
and reset their passwords promptly. 
The solution is implemented by integrating with existing security measures, leveraging advanced 
algorithms, and focusing on usability to empower users to take swift action against potential threats. 
It will be deployed during login attempts, continuously monitoring for unusual patterns to ensure 
proactive prevention. The outcome is expected to include a substantial reduction in unauthorized 
access incidents, improved digital hygiene, and enhanced protection for sensitive personal and 
organizational data. The project ultimately combines technical innovation with user empowerment 
to create a robust and reliable security framework.

1.1 Background and Context 
In a way, cybersecurity has started getting embedded into many modern aspects of living today, 
which are globalizing online platform communication, work, financial transactions, and even for 
storing data. It forms perhaps one of the core most issues from cybersecurity, the protection of user 
credentials. Because login pages usually become those first gateways that essentially lead to 
sensitive information, the landscape of cybersecurity has grown big with an increase in remote 
working, e-commerce, and online services. Unarguably, robust security measures will be crucial 
in avoiding unauthorized access. 
In the very beginning, computer networking was an emerging field and so was the equal threat to 
password security. As technology became better and advances were forged, it started proving the 
old ways of password-based authentication rather ineffective. It was only in the 90s, well into the 
early 2000s, that strong password policies were enforced along with compliance on password 
security. But as the threat of the internet grew and more data breaches were disclosed, came the 
introduction of methods like Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) and Multi-Factor Authentication 
(MFA) which were invented to counter both password theft and reproduction. 
Weak and stolen passwords, credential stuffing, and phishing seem to be the most dangerous 
vectors at this stage. Indeed, it has been determined that 81% of hacking breaches involve weak 
or compromised passwords. More recently, bad actors have started using machine learning and AI 
in order to automate and accelerate traditional password-cracking techniques that defenses have 
become too good at defending against. Such a scenario will require adaptive, relevant solutions 
that respond in real time whenever unauthorized access attempts are made. 
Today, the topic has gained even more critical relevance in the context of a rapidly changing digital 
environment where cyber-attacks are increasingly becoming more frequent and sophisticated. In 
addition to financial losses and data theft, unauthorized access to an email account or to other 
sensitive data has a really deep negative impact on the reputation of individuals and organizations.

1.4 Scope and Limitations 
The project, "Password Cracking Detection and Prevention System with Anomalous Device 
Detection and Response", comprises the conception, realization, and testing of the complete 
security solution that is designed to fortify the protection of an email account against unauthorized 
access. This project base is on the following areas: 
1. Anomaly Detection Mechanism: Designing an advanced algorithm that will monitor login 
patterns and detect suspicious or unfamiliar device logins as they happen. 
2. Automated User Notification System: Create a complete email alert system to notify users 
in real-time whenever strange login events occur along with details about the accessing 
device. 
3. User-Controlled Response Tools: Implementing the feature to users to log out all active 
sessions with prompts to change their password proactively in case of unauthorized access. 
4. Device Tracking and Logging: Develop a system that maintains history records of devices 
commonly used to access the account so that the detection accuracy of anomalies login 
could be enhanced. 
The project would be just on email account safety, majors in detection and user response rather 
than other mechanisms that strengthen passwords or integrated multi-factor authentication (MFA). 
Therefore, this project is designed to significantly improve email account security, but there are 
some inherent limitations and constraints attached to it which may inhibit its implementation or 
outcome:

2.1 Review of Relevant Theories and Concepts 
2.1.1 Password Security and User Authentication 
Theories and concepts related to password security and user authentication are fundamental in the 
field of cybersecurity. The password security and user authentication base is very important in the 
field of cybersecurity (Antonenko, 2022). Password-based authentication systems, being 
widespread, are naturally prone to various forms of attack vectors (Editor, 2022). Amongst these, 
techniques include brute force attacks, which test every possible combination systematically for a 
password, and dictionary attacks, precompiled lists used for efficient breaking of the system 
(Vigliarolo, 2019). The susceptibility of these systems is often compounded by user practices, such 
as weak password selection or reuse across platforms (Inc, 2024). Advanced methods include hash 
cracking, which exploits vulnerabilities in password storage mechanisms, highlighting the 
importance of robust hashing algorithms and multi-factor authentication as key defenses (Cypress 
Data Defense, 2020). 
Understanding these weaknesses highlights the need for effective countermeasures, which this 
research seeks to address. The project focuses on implementing stronger authentication 
mechanisms through Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and advanced password hashing 
techniques. These methods not only minimize the risk of unauthorized access but also provide a 
solid foundation for effective breach identification and response mechanisms.

2.1.2 Anomaly Detection Algorithms 
Anomaly detection algorithms are vital for identifying deviations from expected patterns, serving 
as key components in monitoring and securing systems (Bernard & Stryker, 2023). These 
algorithms use machine learning and statistical models to detect anomalies that might indicate 
security breaches or system faults (Coursera Staff, 2024). 
The research leverages machine learning models, including clustering algorithms, rule-based 
systems, and supervised learning techniques such as neural networks and support vector machines, 
to develop adaptable and precise detection mechanisms (Quiroz-Vázquez, 2024). By training 
models on labeled datasets, these techniques enable the classification of events as normal or 
anomalous. 
The research integrates these advanced methods into the system design to ensure resilience against 
emerging security challenges. By enabling the system to identify threat patterns and respond 
promptly, these mechanisms enhance the precision and flexibility of intrusion detection systems 
(Nailman, 2024). 
2.1.3 User Awareness 
User awareness is critical to effective cybersecurity strategies. The human factor is normally the 
weakest link, where a user may compromise security by being either negligent or lack of awareness 
(Leal, 2022). Research on the psychological and behavioral dimensions of cybersecurity highlights 
the importance of user engagement and education (Grobler et al., 2021). Research has shown that 
users are more responsive to alerts and notifications when they are short, contextually relevant, 
and accompanied by actionable advice (Team, 2022). Proactive measures include regular security 
training and the promotion of best practices that put users in a better position to recognize and 
mitigate potential threats (Ralph, 2023). The culture of security awareness will help an 
organization go a long way in improving its overall security posture.
